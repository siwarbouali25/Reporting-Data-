# Eurolux Universal Bank AG - Climate Disclosure: Data Limitations & Expert-Review Log

**Internal quality-assurance document - not part of the published report.**

For each disclosure section this log records (a) the IFRS S1 / S2 requirement areas that could not be fully satisfied because the underlying data does not contain the relevant information, and (b) residual items flagged for expert review before the report is finalised. Sections are generated under an evidence-bounded policy: where a requirement cannot be supported by the available data it is disclosed as a boundary rather than estimated. None of the items below indicate fabricated content; they indicate where expert judgement and/or additional source data are required.

## General Requirements  
*Automated score: 7/10 - needs human review*

### IFRS S1/S2 requirement areas limited by available data
_No data-driven requirement gaps recorded for this section._

### Items requiring expert correction before finalisation (material)
- [ ] Sources of guidance: 'The available evidence does not document the application or consideration of other sustainability disclosure frameworks or standards (such as SASB...)' conflicts with IFRS S1 expectations to consider SASB and is not supported by evidence (sources_of_guidance is blank). This should be reframed to: 'No documentation was available in the evidence set on whether SASB or other sources were considered/applied.'

### Minor wording for editorial review (optional)
- Fair presentation: 'The Bank aims to present the information in a transparent, balanced and decision‑oriented manner.' This is aspirational and acceptable, but could be tightened to avoid implying a formal policy not evidenced.
- Connected information: trend metrics are described as used to 'illustrate the evolution of oversight over time'—acceptable given trend metrics exist, but clarify that these are the specific trend KPIs available in evidence (not a complete governance effectiveness assessment).
- Materiality assessment: 'risk‑ and portfolio‑driven screening approach' is plausible but not evidenced as a documented process artefact; keep but explicitly label as 'based on available evidence indicators' rather than a formal process.

## Governance  
*Automated score: 8/10 - needs human review*

### IFRS S1/S2 requirement areas limited by available data
_Disclosed in the report as evidence boundaries because the source data does not contain the underlying information. An expert should confirm the boundary wording and supply the data if it exists elsewhere._

- Board charter/mandate boundary: states documentation does not include a separate climate-specific Board charter or formal mandate.
- Committee charter boundary: states documentation does not include ESG & Sustainability Committee charter/terms of reference and describes role based on observed activities.
- Trade-offs boundary: explicitly states decision evidence does not describe quantified/specific Board-level trade-offs (profitability, capital allocation, implementation cost, risk appetite, competing priorities).
- Escalation boundary: states no formal escalation thresholds/trigger-based escalation mechanics evidenced for climate risks.
- Skills adequacy boundary: states no formal process evidenced for Board assessment of adequacy of climate skills/competencies.
- Major transaction checks boundary: states checks are evidenced but no formal mandatory policy or thresholds are described.
- Alignment boundary: treats TCFD/IFRS S2 alignment as source-data flags and states no separate governance control process to verify/assure alignment is evidenced.
- Assurance boundary: correctly limits external assurance to Scope 1 and Scope 2 under ISAE 3000 by EY and clarifies financed emissions/Scope 3 are outside scope.

### Items requiring expert correction before finalisation (material)
- [ ] Management risk register: 'These examples include recorded financial impact estimates' — the compact evidence provides financial impact estimates for the listed material risk examples, but it does not explicitly evidence that such estimates are recorded in the register for all examples as a register attribute; this should be reframed to 'available evidence includes financial impact estimates for selected risks' or similar.

### Minor wording for editorial review (optional)
- Use 'ERM integration flag/indicator (6 of 8 integrated)' rather than implying a broader set of 'ERM integration indicators' unless the underlying documentation explicitly defines indicators.
- Use 'available evidence indicates mitigation actions associated with risks include…' rather than implying the register formally 'records' those actions as a structured register field.
- Avoid implying assurance is part of 'controls' unless clearly separated as external assurance over specific metrics (already mostly done).

## Strategy  
*Automated score: 8/10 - needs human review*

### IFRS S1/S2 requirement areas limited by available data
_Disclosed in the report as evidence boundaries because the source data does not contain the underlying information. An expert should confirm the boundary wording and supply the data if it exists elsewhere._

- Business model: explicitly states that a separate, detailed business-model impact assessment is not available and therefore not provided.
- Trade-offs: explicitly states quantified/documented trade-off analysis is not available.
- Targets: clearly limits disclosure to target summary fields and states that validation/baselines/milestones/gross-net/credit use are not specified.
- Transition plan: states that key assumptions/dependencies are not specified in the Strategy evidence and are not described.
- Scenarios: repeatedly and clearly states scenario outputs are modelled estimates and not realised losses/forecasts.
- Opportunities: states revenue impacts are estimates with confidence levels and not assured outcomes.
- Resilience: bounds resilience statements to NGFS v4 scenarios/assumptions and notes limits on assessing full operational adaptation capacity.

### Items requiring expert correction before finalisation (material)
- [ ] Inconsistent time-horizon definitions (0–2/2–5/>5 vs up to 2025/2030/2050) without explaining how they reconcile. This affects IFRS S1 time-horizon clarity and could mislead users about when effects are expected.

### Minor wording for editorial review (optional)
- Replace 'international net-zero emissions 2050 trajectory' with 'IEA NZE 2050 trajectory' to match the available evidence.
- Clarify that the eight risks are the 'eight material risks' selected from a broader register (evidence profile indicates 16 register entries).
- When discussing hot house scenarios, separate what is evidenced for NDC vs what is assumed/used for Current Policies, or state that detailed carbon-price ramp assumptions are only evidenced for NDC.

## Risk Management  
*Automated score: 8/10 - needs human review*

### IFRS S1/S2 requirement areas limited by available data
_Disclosed in the report as evidence boundaries because the source data does not contain the underlying information. An expert should confirm the boundary wording and supply the data if it exists elsewhere._

- No formal climate risk appetite thresholds are evidenced; the draft explicitly states it does not describe quantitative climate risk appetite limits.
- No formal climate-risk-specific policy documents or formal escalation thresholds/triggers are evidenced; the draft discloses these are not available in the source data.
- No detailed controls/procedures (eg underwriting, pricing, ECL/provisioning integration) are evidenced; the draft states mitigation actions are presented as risk-specific register entries rather than a complete control framework.
- Value chain coverage is constrained: no distinct upstream climate risk entries and no purely operational climate risk entries are recorded; the draft discloses this limitation.
- Scenario analysis outputs are described as modelled, forward-looking inputs and not realised losses; the draft makes this explicit and avoids implying portfolio-wide application.

### Items requiring expert correction before finalisation (material)
- [ ] “Mitigation actions … include: … collateral revaluation and flood mapping overlays” (plural ‘overlays’) overstates what is evidenced (singular ‘overlay’).

### Minor wording for editorial review (optional)
- “provide additional context … and are used as monitoring context” — tighten to “provide contextual information” unless there is explicit evidence that KPIs are used in monitoring routines.
- “updated when assessments are revised” — evidence supports that 4 risks are marked ‘changed since prior period’, but not the trigger/discipline for updates; tighten to “the register records changes to assessments for four risks versus the prior period.”
- “internal operational climate risk management … reflected indirectly through … business continuity arrangements” — acceptable as a boundary narrative, but should be clearly framed as inference rather than evidenced practice.

## Metrics and Targets  
*Automated score: 8/10 - needs human review*

### IFRS S1/S2 requirement areas limited by available data
_Disclosed in the report as evidence boundaries because the source data does not contain the underlying information. An expert should confirm the boundary wording and supply the data if it exists elsewhere._

- Operational GHG: states detailed 2024 Scope 1, 2 and 3 operational emissions records are unavailable and therefore no quantified 2024 operational emissions are reported.

### Items requiring expert correction before finalisation (material)

### Minor wording for editorial review (optional)

- Green loans definition: 'based on internal criteria and/or applicable regulatory or market taxonomies' is plausible but not evidenced; consider framing as 'classified as green in internal records; criteria not described in available documentation'.
