# Eurolux Universal Bank AG - Climate Disclosure: Data Limitations & Expert-Review Log

**Internal quality-assurance document - not part of the published report.**

For each disclosure section this log records (a) the IFRS S1 / S2 requirement areas that could not be fully satisfied because the underlying data does not contain the relevant information, and (b) residual items flagged for expert review before the report is finalised. Sections are generated under an evidence-bounded policy: where a requirement cannot be supported by the available data it is disclosed as a boundary rather than estimated. None of the items below indicate fabricated content; they indicate where expert judgement and/or additional source data are required.

### Items requiring expert correction before finalisation (material)
- [ ] Sources of guidance: The requirements pack expects identification of other guidance applied (e.g., SASB consideration). The draft states it does not identify other standards beyond IFRS S1/S2; however, elsewhere in the report (Metrics and targets / financed emissions / scenario analysis) it is highly likely that external methodologies are used. Without explicit confirmation, this creates a material risk of omission/misstatement. Either (a) explicitly state that no other external standards were applied, or (b) list the specific ones actually used (e.g., PCAF for financed emissions, GHG Protocol for Scopes 1–3, scenario providers) if they are used in the underlying sections.

### Minor wording for editorial review (optional)
- Fair presentation: 'The Bank aims to prepare this report in a transparent, balanced and decision-useful manner.' (Fine, but avoid implying neutrality/faithful representation beyond what is supported; current wording is mostly aspirational.)
- Connected information: 'The Bank aims to ensure that the data and assumptions ... are ... consistent with those used in preparing the related financial statements...' (Acceptable as an aim; avoid implying achieved consistency without specifying what is actually aligned.)
- Timing and location: 'The disclosures form part of ... dedicated sustainability and climate-related financial reporting for the period.' (Acceptable, but could clarify whether this is a standalone report or part of another publication; draft already notes publication date/location not specified.)

## Governance  
*Automated score: 8/10 - needs human review*

### IFRS S1/S2 requirement areas limited by available data
_Disclosed in the report as evidence boundaries because the source data does not contain the underlying information. An expert should confirm the boundary wording and supply the data if it exists elsewhere._

- “Six of these 8 recorded climate-related risks carry an indicator that they are integrated into the enterprise risk management framework; enterprise-wide integration into the enterprise risk management framework is not evidenced.”

- “The Bank does not currently disclose whether these committees represent a continuous or renamed structure.”
- “These are alignment indicators and do not constitute a separate governance control process designed specifically to verify or assure that alignment...”

### Items requiring expert correction before finalisation (material)
- [ ] “As an illustration, the register includes a high-rated physical risk related to mortgage book flood exposure ... with mitigation actions that include engagement and transition-plan covenants with borrowers.” (the specific risk example and 24.8 million euros impact are evidenced, but the draft adds ‘transition-plan covenants’ wording; the evidenced mitigation action is “Engagement and transition-plan covenants with borrowers” and should be reproduced exactly or paraphrased without adding specificity beyond the label).
- [ ] “The Bank obtained limited external assurance from EY over its Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions for 2024, in accordance with ISAE 3000.” (provider, limited assurance, and ISAE3000 are evidenced; however, stating assurance is ‘over its Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions’ is acceptable only if the assurance scope is exactly emissions—evidence says “Scope 1 and 2 emissions” but does not specify whether it covers metrics, methodology, or disclosures; tighten to ‘limited assurance over reported Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions’ to avoid overstating breadth).

### Minor wording for editorial review (optional)
- Replace “integration indicators within the enterprise risk framework” with evidence-anchored wording (eg, “an ERM-integration flag in the climate risk register”).
- Replace “used to track” with “reported as indicators” unless the Bank can evidence internal use in governance monitoring.
- Avoid implying a formal policy from “performed climate checks for major transactions”; keep as “the Bank performed climate checks for major transactions in 2024” (already used elsewhere).
- “Scenario analysis identifiers are recorded for selected risks, linking them to specific climate scenarios.” is supported at a high level; consider adding “for selected risks” (already present) and avoid implying comprehensive coverage.

## Strategy  
*Automated score: 8/10 - needs human review*

### IFRS S1/S2 requirement areas limited by available data
_Disclosed in the report as evidence boundaries because the source data does not contain the underlying information. An expert should confirm the boundary wording and supply the data if it exists elsewhere._

- The Bank does not currently disclose a separate, detailed business-model impact assessment that isolates climate-related effects from other strategic drivers.
- Quantified trade-off analysis between climate-related and other strategic objectives has not been documented in this Strategy section / is not included.
- Climate capex and opex are not presented as separate line items in the primary financial statements and an activity-level breakdown is not currently disclosed.
- The Bank does not currently quantify the specific current-period effects of climate-related risks and opportunities on financial position, performance and cash flows, and does not provide granular mapping to financial statement line items.
- Only target summary information is used in this Strategy section; detailed assumptions, baselines, milestones, validation evidence and carbon credit use are not described here.
- Scenario outputs (losses, stranded assets, revenue at risk) are modelled estimates and not realised or forecast losses; opportunity revenue impacts are estimates with uncertainty.
- The Bank does not currently disclose sufficient detail to assess full operational adaptation capacity across all business lines.

### Items requiring expert correction before finalisation (material)
- [ ] “The Bank has set climate-related targets … using frameworks such as a 1.5°C pathway and the Net-Zero Banking Alliance as references.” (Must be tightened to the specific frameworks evidenced per target and avoid implying additional frameworks or validation beyond what is disclosed.)

### Minor wording for editorial review (optional)
- “Scenario analysis using NGFS v4 pathways is used to test the resilience of the Bank’s strategy and to quantify modelled financial impacts…” (Tighten to clarify scope is primarily the lending book and that outputs are modelled estimates subject to uncertainty—this is mostly done later but could be consistent upfront.)
- “Under this scenario, modelled stranded assets in 2050 are estimated at EUR 33,575.2 million.” (Clarify this is an estimate derived from sector-level fossil fuel capex exposure and is not a balance-sheet impairment estimate.)
- “Near-term resilience is acceptable…” (Already scenario-bounded, but could be tightened to “within the parameters of this scenario and the Bank’s current capital framework”.)

## Risk Management  
*Automated score: 8/10 - needs human review*

### IFRS S1/S2 requirement areas limited by available data
_Disclosed in the report as evidence boundaries because the source data does not contain the underlying information. An expert should confirm the boundary wording and supply the data if it exists elsewhere._

- The Bank does not currently disclose formal climate risk appetite thresholds (quantitative limits/tolerances).
- The Bank does not currently disclose detailed climate risk policies, formal control procedures, roles/responsibilities, approval workflows or lines of defence for climate risk management.
- The Bank does not currently disclose formal escalation thresholds or triggers for climate-related risks.
- Upstream climate-related risks (suppliers/procurement/funding markets) are not included/quantified in the 2024 climate risk register and are outside the scope of the current disclosures.
- Internal operational climate risks are not separately identified/quantified in the 2024 climate risk register and are outside the scope of the current disclosures.
- Scenario analysis outputs are modelled estimates and not realised losses; uncertainty in assumptions is acknowledged.

### Items requiring expert correction before finalisation (material)
- [ ] “Mitigation actions recorded for this risk include engagement and transition-plan covenants with borrowers” (for mortgage flood risk): transition-plan covenants are not evidenced as effective adaptation/physical-risk mitigants; without clarifying how these covenants address flood exposure, this reads as an over-assertive control/mitigation claim.
- [ ] “Climate scenario stress testing integrated into ICAAP” presented as a mitigation action: integration into ICAAP is evidenced as a register action, but describing it as a mitigation (risk reduction) can be misleading unless reframed as an assessment/measurement enhancement rather than a control that reduces risk.

### Minor wording for editorial review (optional)
- “The climate risk register is the central tool through which the Bank organises its climate-related risk management processes.” (acceptable, but could be softened to avoid implying completeness of processes beyond what is described).
- “These assessments provide a basis for prioritising management attention…” (fine, but could clarify that prioritisation is based on register ratings/impacts rather than a broader governance process).
- Use of the word “guarantees” appears only in a negation (“not presented… as guarantees of risk reduction”) and is acceptable; consider replacing with “assurances” or removing the term to satisfy the deterministic warning.
