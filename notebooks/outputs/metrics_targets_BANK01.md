### Metrics and targets

#### Overview

This section presents the key metrics and targets that Eurolux Universal Bank AG uses to monitor climate-related risks and opportunities across its operations and financing activities. The focus is on portfolio emissions, exposure to high‑carbon and fossil‑fuel sectors, green lending, climate-related capital and operating expenditure, and climate targets.

The Bank distinguishes between:
- sustainability-related metrics that inform the overall risk profile and strategic positioning;  
- climate-specific metrics, including financed emissions and portfolio carbon intensity; and  
- climate targets that guide the transition of both operations and the financed portfolio.

The primary quantitative indicators for 2024 are financed emissions, portfolio carbon intensity, sectoral exposure metrics, and climate-related financial allocations, together with progress indicators from the Bank’s climate target records.

#### Sustainability-related metrics and targets

This subsection summarises the core sustainability-related metrics that the Bank uses to assess and manage climate-related risks and opportunities in its banking activities.

Key portfolio and exposure metrics for 2024 are:

- Total assets: EUR 850,000 million.  
- Total loans: EUR 31,150.64 million.

Within this balance sheet context, the Bank monitors the following climate-relevant portfolio indicators:

- High‑carbon sector exposure:  
  - EUR 11,059.49 million of loans to sectors classified internally as high carbon.  
  - This represents 35.5% of the total loan portfolio.

- Fossil‑fuel exposure:  
  - EUR 6,899.59 million of loans to fossil‑fuel-related activities.  
  - This represents 22.15% of the total loan portfolio.

- Green loans:  
  - EUR 3,335.75 million of loans classified as green lending.  
  - This represents 10.71% of the total loan portfolio.

These metrics are used to monitor the Bank’s exposure to transition risk, to inform sectoral risk appetite, and to support the development of green and transition finance offerings. They also provide the basis for tracking progress against the Bank’s climate-related targets described later in this section.

#### Greenhouse gas emissions

This subsection focuses on the Bank’s climate-related emissions metrics, with an emphasis on financed emissions and portfolio carbon intensity.

The Bank does not currently disclose Scope 1, Scope 2 or Scope 3 operational greenhouse gas emissions for the reporting year. The climate-related emissions metrics presented in this section therefore relate to financed emissions from the lending portfolio.

#### Managing exposure towards financed emissions

This subsection presents the Bank’s 2024 financed emissions metrics and related portfolio indicators, which are central to managing climate-related risks in the lending book.

For 2024, the Bank reports the following financed emissions metrics for its loan portfolio:

- Financed emissions from lending: 35,973,167.69 tCO₂e.  
- Carbon intensity of lending: 1,154.81 tCO₂e per EUR million of loans, calculated using total loans of EUR 31,150.64 million as the denominator.

These metrics are used to monitor the emissions profile of the financed portfolio and to support strategic decisions on sectoral allocation, client engagement and product development. They also serve as the primary performance indicators for the Bank’s financed-emissions intensity reduction target.

#### Climate-related financial metrics and resource allocation

This subsection discloses climate-related financial metrics that indicate how the Bank allocates capital and operating expenditure to support its climate strategy and manage climate-related risks and opportunities.

For 2024, the Bank reports the following climate-related financial indicators:

- Climate-related capital expenditure:  
  - EUR 476.95 million of capital expenditure directed to climate-related initiatives, including investments intended to support the decarbonisation of operations and the development of climate-related products and services.

- Climate-related operating expenditure:  
  - EUR 219.32 million of operating expenditure related to climate initiatives, including activities such as climate risk management, data and systems enhancements, and climate-related training and engagement.

- Green lending and sectoral allocation:  
  - Green loans of EUR 3,335.75 million, representing 10.71% of the total loan portfolio.  
  - High‑carbon sector exposure of EUR 11,059.49 million (35.5% of total loans).  
  - Fossil‑fuel exposure of EUR 6,899.59 million (22.15% of total loans).

These financial metrics are used to monitor the Bank’s resource allocation towards climate-related priorities, to assess the balance between high‑carbon and green exposures, and to inform capital planning and product strategy.

#### Climate targets and progress

This subsection summarises the Bank’s climate-related targets and reported progress. All quantitative parameters described below are target parameters and do not represent current-year operational emissions. Progress indicators are reported from internal target records.

The Bank has three principal climate-related targets recorded for 2024:

1. **Operational Scope 1 and 2 absolute emissions reduction target**  
   - Target type and scope: Absolute reduction target covering Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions for the whole entity.  
   - Metric and gases: Expressed in tonnes of CO₂ equivalent and covering all seven Kyoto greenhouse gases.  
   - Baseline and target years:  
     - Baseline year: 2020.  
     - Target year: 2030.  
   - Baseline and target parameters:  
     - Baseline of 137,849 tCO₂e in 2020.  
     - A 42% reduction in emissions by 2030 relative to the 2020 baseline, on a gross basis.  
   - Interim milestones:  
     - 120,480 tCO₂e in 2025.  
     - 97,321.4 tCO₂e in 2028.  
     - These values are target parameters and are not reported as projected or actual emissions for those years.  
   - Framework and external reference:  
     - Target framework identified as a 1.5°C pathway under the Science Based Targets initiative, and recorded as validated by that initiative in the Bank’s target records.  
   - Governance and review:  
     - The target applies to the whole entity and is reviewed on a triennial basis. Oversight of climate-related targets by the Board and relevant Board-level committees is described in the Governance section of this report.  
   - Reported progress:  
     - Reported progress of 40.0% towards the 2030 reduction objective as at 2024, using an absolute tCO₂e metric. This percentage is an internal progress indicator.

2. **Financed emissions intensity reduction target for lending**  
   - Target type and scope: Intensity reduction target for financed emissions, covering Scope 3, category 15 (financed emissions) for the whole entity.  
   - Metric and gases: Expressed in tonnes of CO₂ equivalent per EUR million of lending and covering all seven Kyoto greenhouse gases.  
   - Baseline and target years:  
     - Baseline year: 2022.  
     - Target year: 2030.  
   - Baseline and target parameters:  
     - Baseline intensity of 20.0 tCO₂e per EUR million of lending in 2022.  
     - A 30% reduction in financed emissions intensity by 2030 relative to the 2022 baseline, on a gross basis.  
   - Interim milestones:  
     - 18.2 tCO₂e per EUR million of lending in 2025.  
     - 15.8 tCO₂e per EUR million of lending in 2028.  
     - These values are target parameters and are not reported as projected or actual intensity values for those years.  
   - Framework and external reference:  
     - Target framework identified as the Net‑Zero Banking Alliance, with the United Nations Environment Programme Finance Initiative identified in the Bank’s target records as the coordinating body for this alliance.  
   - Sectoral approach and review:  
     - A sectoral decarbonisation approach is used and the target is reviewed annually. Oversight of climate-related targets by the Board and relevant Board-level committees is described in the Governance section of this report.  
   - Reported progress:  
     - Reported progress of 25.0% towards the 2030 intensity reduction objective as at 2024, using a tCO₂e per EUR million metric. This percentage is an internal progress indicator.

3. **Long-term net‑zero greenhouse gas target**  
   - Target type and scope: Net‑zero target covering all greenhouse gas emission scopes for the whole entity.  
   - Metric and gases: Expressed in tonnes of CO₂ equivalent and covering all seven Kyoto greenhouse gases.  
   - Baseline and target years:  
     - Baseline year: 2020.  
     - Target year: 2050.  
   - Baseline and target parameters:  
     - Baseline of 120,000 tCO₂e in 2020.  
     - A 100% reduction in net emissions by 2050 relative to the 2020 baseline.  
   - Relationship to gross targets:  
     - Associated with the gross emissions reduction target for operational Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions described above.  
   - Framework and external reference:  
     - Target framework identified as the Net‑Zero Banking Alliance, with the United Nations Environment Programme Finance Initiative identified in the Bank’s target records as the coordinating body for this alliance.  
   - Planned use of carbon credits:  
     - Up to 8.2% of the reduction towards net zero may be met through the use of carbon credits, with a focus on technology-based removal credits. This parameter is recorded at target level and does not represent a current commitment to procure a specific volume of credits.  
   - Governance and review:  
     - The target is reviewed on a biennial basis. Oversight of climate-related targets by the Board and relevant Board-level committees is described in the Governance section of this report.  
   - Reported progress:  
     - Reported progress of 13.3% towards the 2050 net‑zero objective as at 2024, expressed as a percentage reduction versus the baseline. This percentage is an internal progress indicator.

Summary of climate targets

The Bank’s climate targets collectively cover operational emissions (Scope 1 and Scope 2), financed emissions intensity (Scope 3, category 15), and a long-term net‑zero objective across all scopes. The target frameworks referenced include the Science Based Targets initiative 1.5°C pathway and the Net‑Zero Banking Alliance. References to external initiatives and coordinating bodies relate to the design and registration of the targets and do not extend to verification of current-year emissions, progress indicators or future achievement.

#### Metrics and targets limitations and evidence boundaries

This subsection summarises the key limitations and boundaries that apply to the metrics and targets disclosed above.

- **Scope of climate metrics**: The metrics presented focus on financed emissions from the lending portfolio, portfolio exposure indicators and climate-related financial allocations. Operational greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3) are not disclosed for the reporting year.  
- **Financed emissions metrics**: Financed emissions and carbon intensity metrics are presented for the lending portfolio, with intensity calculated using total loans as the denominator. No further breakdown by asset class, sector or geography is provided in this report.  
- **Comparability and methodology**: The Bank aims to apply consistent definitions and calculation approaches for climate-related metrics and targets over time. Where methodologies are refined or new metrics are introduced, the Bank will explain material changes and, where practicable, provide revised comparative information in future reporting. Detailed calculation methodologies, data quality assessments and underlying assumptions for the metrics disclosed in this section are not described in this report.  
- **Target parameters and progress indicators**: All baseline values, reduction percentages, interim milestones, progress percentages, frameworks and references to external initiatives described in the climate targets subsection are parameters of the Bank’s climate targets. These parameters do not represent current-year operational emissions and do not constitute a guarantee that the targets will be met.
