## General Requirements

### Objective and scope
These sustainability-related financial disclosures are intended to provide information about sustainability-related risks and opportunities that could reasonably be expected to affect the entity’s cash flows, its access to finance or cost of capital over the short, medium or long term (collectively, sustainability-related risks and opportunities that could reasonably be expected to affect the entity’s prospects). (IFRS S1.3)

### Fair presentation
A complete set of sustainability-related financial disclosures presents fairly all sustainability-related risks and opportunities that could reasonably be expected to affect the entity’s prospects. (IFRS S1.11)

Fair presentation requires disclosure of relevant information about those sustainability-related risks and opportunities and faithful representation in accordance with the principles set out in IFRS S1. To achieve faithful representation, the disclosures provide a complete, neutral and accurate depiction of those sustainability-related risks and opportunities. (IFRS S1.13)

Fair presentation also requires:

- information that is comparable, verifiable, timely and understandable; and (IFRS S1.15(a))
- additional information when compliance with specifically applicable requirements in IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards is insufficient to enable users of general purpose financial reports to understand the effects of sustainability-related risks and opportunities on the entity’s cash flows, its access to finance and cost of capital over the short, medium and long term. (IFRS S1.15(b))

### Identification of sustainability-related risks and opportunities
To identify sustainability-related risks and opportunities that could reasonably be expected to affect the entity’s prospects, the entity applies paragraphs B1–B12 of IFRS S1. (IFRS S1.12)

In identifying sustainability-related risks and opportunities that could reasonably be expected to affect the entity’s prospects, the entity applies IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards. (IFRS S1.54)

### Material information
The entity discloses material information about the sustainability-related risks and opportunities that could reasonably be expected to affect the entity’s prospects. (IFRS S1.17)

To identify and disclose material information, the entity applies paragraphs B13–B37 of IFRS S1. (IFRS S1.19)

### Reporting entity and connected information
The sustainability-related financial disclosures are for the same reporting entity as the related financial statements. (IFRS S1.20)

The entity provides information in a manner that enables users of general purpose financial reports to understand:

- connections between the items to which the information relates, such as connections between various sustainability-related risks and opportunities that could reasonably be expected to affect the entity’s prospects; (IFRS S1.21(a))
- connections between disclosures provided within the sustainability-related financial disclosures, such as connections between disclosures on governance, strategy, risk management and metrics and targets; and (IFRS S1.21(b)(i))
- connections across the sustainability-related financial disclosures and other general purpose financial reports published by the entity, such as the related financial statements. (IFRS S1.21(b)(ii))

The entity identifies the financial statements to which the sustainability-related financial disclosures relate. (IFRS S1.22)

Data and assumptions used in preparing the sustainability-related financial disclosures are consistent, to the extent possible considering the requirements of IFRS Accounting Standards or other applicable GAAP, with the corresponding data and assumptions used in preparing the related financial statements. (IFRS S1.23)

When currency is specified as the unit of measure in the sustainability-related financial disclosures, the entity uses the presentation currency of its related financial statements. (IFRS S1.24)

### Core content
Unless another IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standard permits or requires otherwise in specified circumstances, the entity provides disclosures about:

- **Governance** — the governance processes, controls and procedures used to monitor and manage sustainability-related risks and opportunities; (IFRS S1.25(a))
- **Strategy** — the approach used to manage sustainability-related risks and opportunities; (IFRS S1.25(b))
- **Risk management** — the processes used to identify, assess, prioritise and monitor sustainability-related risks and opportunities; and (IFRS S1.25(c))
- **Metrics and targets** — performance in relation to sustainability-related risks and opportunities, including progress towards any targets the entity has set or is required to meet by law or regulation. (IFRS S1.25(d))

### Sources of guidance
In identifying applicable disclosure requirements about a sustainability-related risk or opportunity that could reasonably be expected to affect the entity’s prospects, the entity applies the IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standard that specifically applies to that sustainability-related risk or opportunity. (IFRS S1.56)

In addition to IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards, the entity refers to and considers the applicability of the disclosure topics in the SASB Standards. (IFRS S1.55(a))

In the absence of an IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standard that specifically applies to a sustainability-related risk or opportunity, the entity applies judgement to identify information that:

- is relevant to the decision-making of users of general purpose financial reports; and (IFRS S1.57(a))
- faithfully represents that sustainability-related risk or opportunity. (IFRS S1.57(b))

In making that judgement, the entity refers to and considers the applicability of the metrics associated with the disclosure topics included in the SASB Standards. (IFRS S1.58(a))

The entity identifies:

- the specific standards, pronouncements, industry practice and other sources of guidance applied in preparing the sustainability-related financial disclosures, including, if applicable, the disclosure topics in the SASB Standards; and (IFRS S1.59(a))
- the industry(s) specified in the IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards, the SASB Standards or other sources of guidance relating to a particular industry(s) applied in preparing the sustainability-related financial disclosures, including in identifying applicable metrics. (IFRS S1.59(b))

### Location of disclosures
Information required by an IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standard may be included in sustainability-related financial disclosures by cross-reference to another report published by the entity. If information is included by cross-reference, the entity applies the requirements in paragraphs B45–B47 of IFRS S1. (IFRS S1.63)
