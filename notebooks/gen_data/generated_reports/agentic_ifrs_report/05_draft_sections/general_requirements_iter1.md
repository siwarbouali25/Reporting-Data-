## 1. General Requirements

### 1.0 How to read this section (basis of preparation)
This section explains the basis on which we prepare and present our sustainability-related financial disclosures, including how we determine what to disclose, how we ensure the information is connected to our financial statements and other general purpose financial reports, and how the disclosures are organised across the core content pillars required by IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards.

Key preparation principles applied in preparing these sustainability-related financial disclosures include:

- **Relevance and materiality**—we focus on information that is material to users’ decisions.
- **Faithful representation**—we aim for complete, neutral and accurate information, including appropriate explanation of uncertainty.
- **Connectivity**—we present information so users can understand linkages within the sustainability disclosures and to the related financial statements.
- **Consistency**—we align data, assumptions and (where applicable) currency with the related financial statements to the extent possible.

(See [Appendix: Methodology] and [Section: Governance], [Section: Strategy], [Section: Risk management], and [Section: Metrics and targets].)

### 1.1 Objective and scope
**Purpose:** to explain what our sustainability-related financial disclosures are designed to help users assess.

Our sustainability-related financial disclosures are intended to provide information about sustainability-related risks and opportunities that could reasonably be expected to affect our cash flows, our access to finance or our cost of capital over the short, medium or long term (that is, risks and opportunities that could reasonably be expected to affect our prospects). (IFRS S1.3)

### 1.2 Fair presentation
**Purpose:** to describe how we identify relevant sustainability-related risks and opportunities and when we provide additional information to achieve fair presentation.

To identify sustainability-related risks and opportunities that could reasonably be expected to affect our prospects, we apply IFRS S1 paragraphs B1–B12. (IFRS S1.12)

We provide additional information when meeting the specifically applicable requirements in IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards would not, on its own, enable users of general purpose financial reports to understand the effects of sustainability-related risks and opportunities on our cash flows, access to finance and cost of capital over the short, medium and long term. (IFRS S1.15(b))

### 1.3 Materiality
**Purpose:** to explain how we determine which sustainability-related information is included.

We disclose material information about sustainability-related risks and opportunities that could reasonably be expected to affect our prospects. (IFRS S1.17)

In identifying and disclosing material information, we apply IFRS S1 paragraphs B13–B37. (IFRS S1.19)

### 1.4 Reporting entity
**Purpose:** to clarify the reporting boundary used for sustainability-related financial disclosures.

Our sustainability-related financial disclosures are prepared for the same reporting entity as the related financial statements. (IFRS S1.20)

### 1.5 Connected information
**Purpose:** to help users understand how sustainability-related information links together and to the financial statements.

We present information in a way that enables users of general purpose financial reports to understand connections:

- **within** our sustainability-related financial disclosures (including connections between governance, strategy, risk management, and metrics and targets); and (IFRS S1.21(b)(i))
- **across** our sustainability-related financial disclosures and other general purpose financial reports we publish, including the related financial statements. (IFRS S1.21(b)(ii))

We identify the financial statements to which these sustainability-related financial disclosures relate. (IFRS S1.22)

To the extent possible (considering the requirements of IFRS Accounting Standards or other applicable GAAP), we use data and assumptions that are consistent with those used in preparing the related financial statements. (IFRS S1.23)

When we specify currency as a unit of measure in these sustainability-related financial disclosures, we use the presentation currency of the related financial statements. (IFRS S1.24)

### 1.6 Core content
**Purpose:** to explain how our disclosures are structured to meet IFRS S1’s core content requirements.

Unless another IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standard permits or requires otherwise in specified circumstances, we provide disclosures across the following core content areas:

- **Governance**—the governance processes, controls and procedures used to monitor and manage sustainability-related risks and opportunities. (IFRS S1.25(a))
- **Strategy**—how sustainability-related risks and opportunities affect our strategy and decision-making. (IFRS S1.25(b))
- **Risk management**—the processes used to identify, assess, prioritise and monitor sustainability-related risks and opportunities. (IFRS S1.25(c))
- **Metrics and targets**—our performance in relation to sustainability-related risks and opportunities, including progress towards any targets we set or are required to meet by law or regulation. (IFRS S1.25(d))

(Refer to [Section: Governance], [Section: Strategy], [Section: Risk management], and [Section: Metrics and targets].)

### 1.7 Sources of guidance
**Purpose:** to explain which standards and other sources we use to identify relevant topics and metrics.

In identifying sustainability-related risks and opportunities that could reasonably be expected to affect our prospects, we apply IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards. (IFRS S1.54)

We also refer to and consider the applicability of the disclosure topics in the SASB Standards. (IFRS S1.55(a))

When a specific IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standard applies to a sustainability-related risk or opportunity, we apply that Standard’s disclosure requirements. (IFRS S1.56)

When no IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standard specifically applies to a sustainability-related risk or opportunity, we use judgement to identify information that is relevant to users’ decision-making and faithfully represents the risk or opportunity. (IFRS S1.57(a), IFRS S1.57(b))

In making that judgement, we refer to and consider the applicability of the metrics associated with the disclosure topics included in the SASB Standards. (IFRS S1.58(a))

We identify:

- the specific standards, pronouncements, industry practice and other sources of guidance applied in preparing these sustainability-related financial disclosures (including, if applicable, SASB disclosure topics); and (IFRS S1.59(a))
- the industry(s) specified in IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards, the SASB Standards or other sources of guidance that we applied in preparing these sustainability-related financial disclosures, including in identifying applicable metrics. (IFRS S1.59(b))

### 1.8 Location of disclosures
**Purpose:** to explain where required information is presented and how cross-references are used.

Information required by an IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standard may be included in our sustainability-related financial disclosures by cross-reference to another report we publish. When we use cross-references, we apply IFRS S1 paragraphs B45–B47. (IFRS S1.63)

### 1.9 Timing of reporting
**Purpose:** to explain the reporting period covered and how we deal with events after the reporting date.

We report our sustainability-related financial disclosures at the same time as the related financial statements, and the sustainability-related financial disclosures cover the same reporting period as the related financial statements. (IFRS S1.64)

If, after the end of the reporting period but before the date on which the sustainability-related financial disclosures are authorised for issue, we receive information about conditions that existed at the end of the reporting period, we update disclosures that relate to those conditions in light of the new information. (IFRS S1.67)

**Subsequent events:** if events occur after the end of the reporting period but before authorisation for issue, and non-disclosure of those events could influence users’ decisions, we disclose those material events. (IFRS S1.68)

**Interim reporting:** if we are required, or choose, to publish sustainability-related financial disclosures for an interim period, we apply the requirements in IFRS S1 paragraph B48. (IFRS S1.69)

### 1.10 Comparative information
**Purpose:** to support users’ analysis of trends and changes over time.

We provide comparative information for all amounts disclosed in the reporting period. We also provide comparative information for narrative and descriptive disclosures when it is useful for understanding the current period’s sustainability-related financial disclosures. (IFRS S1.70)

### 1.11 Significant judgements
**Purpose:** to help users understand the key judgements that shape the disclosures.

We disclose the judgements—other than those involving estimations—that have the most significant effect on the sustainability-related financial disclosures. (IFRS S1.74)

### 1.12 Measurement uncertainty
**Purpose:** to explain where measurement uncertainty could affect users’ interpretation of disclosed amounts.

We identify amounts in the sustainability-related financial disclosures that are subject to a high level of measurement uncertainty. (IFRS S1.78(a))

For those amounts, we disclose the sources of measurement uncertainty. (IFRS S1.78(b)(i))

### 1.13 Prior period errors
**Purpose:** to explain how we correct material errors and maintain comparability.

If we identify a material prior period error, we restate comparative information unless restatement is impracticable. (IFRS S1.83)

When a material prior period error is identified, we apply IFRS S1 paragraphs B55–B59. (IFRS S1.86)

### 1.14 Closing connectivity statement
This basis of preparation is intended to support clear navigation and connectivity across our disclosures under Governance, Strategy, Risk management, and Metrics and targets, and to link sustainability-related information to the related financial statements where relevant.
