## 2 Strategy

This Strategy section explains how sustainability-related risks and opportunities are considered in strategic decision-making and financial planning, and how they could affect the entity’s business model, value chain, and financial statements over the short, medium and long term.

### 2.1 Time horizons

This subsection defines the time horizons used in these disclosures.

The entity defines its time horizons as follows, aligned to the planning horizons used for strategic decision-making:

- **Short term**: up to 12 months.
- **Medium term**: 1–3 years.
- **Long term**: more than 3 years.

### 2.2 Business model and value chain

This subsection describes where sustainability-related risks and opportunities could affect the entity across its business model and value chain.

The entity’s disclosures in this section are intended to cover:

- the **current and anticipated effects** of sustainability-related risks and opportunities on the entity’s business model and value chain; and
- where in the business model and value chain those risks and opportunities are **concentrated** (for example, by geography, facility, or asset type).

Refer to **[Section: Risk Management]** for how the entity identifies and assesses sustainability-related risks and opportunities.

### 2.3 Effects on strategy and decision-making

This subsection describes how sustainability-related risks and opportunities are reflected in strategy and decision-making.

The entity’s disclosures in this section are intended to cover:

- how the entity has responded to, and plans to respond to, sustainability-related risks and opportunities in its strategy and decision-making;
- progress against plans disclosed in previous reporting periods (including quantitative and qualitative information, where applicable); and
- material trade-offs considered between sustainability-related risks and opportunities and other strategic objectives.

Refer to **[Section: Governance]** for oversight of sustainability-related strategy.

### 2.4 Resilience

This subsection addresses the resilience of the entity’s strategy and business model to sustainability-related risks.

The entity’s disclosures in this section are intended to describe the resilience of its strategy and business model to sustainability-related risks, including (where applicable) the use of scenario analysis required by other IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards.

### 2.5 Financial position, financial performance and cash flows

This subsection addresses the current and anticipated financial effects of sustainability-related risks and opportunities.

The entity’s disclosures in this section are intended to enable users to understand:

- the effects of sustainability-related risks and opportunities on the entity’s financial position, financial performance and cash flows for the reporting period (**current financial effects**); and
- the anticipated effects on the entity’s financial position, financial performance and cash flows over the **short, medium and long term**, taking into consideration how sustainability-related risks and opportunities are included in the entity’s financial planning (**anticipated financial effects**).

The entity’s disclosures in this section are intended to include quantitative and qualitative information about:

- how sustainability-related risks and opportunities affected the entity’s financial position, financial performance and cash flows for the reporting period;
- the sustainability-related risks and opportunities for which there is a significant risk of a material adjustment within the next annual reporting period to the carrying amounts of assets and liabilities reported in the related financial statements;
- how the entity expects its financial position to change over the short, medium and long term, given its strategy to manage sustainability-related risks and opportunities, including:
  - investment and disposal plans (including plans not contractually committed to); and
  - planned sources of funding to implement its strategy; and
- how the entity expects its financial performance and cash flows to change over the short, medium and long term, given its strategy to manage sustainability-related risks and opportunities.

In preparing disclosures about anticipated financial effects, the entity intends to use reasonable and supportable information available at the reporting date without undue cost or effort, and an approach commensurate with the skills, capabilities and resources available for preparing those disclosures.

Refer to **[Financial Statements: Notes – Significant judgements and estimates]** and **[Financial Statements: Notes – Provisions and contingencies]** for related financial statement impacts and estimation uncertainty.
