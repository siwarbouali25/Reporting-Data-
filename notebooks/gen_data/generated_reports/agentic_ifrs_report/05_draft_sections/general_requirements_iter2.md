## 1. General Requirements

### 1.1 Basis of preparation
The disclosures are prepared with reference to IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards requirements evidenced in internal governance documentation.

Internal governance documentation includes references to IFRS S2 paragraph-level considerations recorded in board materials (e.g., §6(a)(iii) and §6(a)(v)).

### 1.2 Statement of alignment with IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards
The organisation has indicated alignment with IFRS S2 in governance-related disclosures.

### 1.3 Governance evidence supporting preparation and oversight
The following internal evidence items support the preparation and oversight context for these disclosures:

- Board materials include references to IFRS S2 paragraph-level requirements (including §6(a)(iii) and §6(a)(v)).
- Governance records include multiple instances marked as IFRS S2 aligned.
