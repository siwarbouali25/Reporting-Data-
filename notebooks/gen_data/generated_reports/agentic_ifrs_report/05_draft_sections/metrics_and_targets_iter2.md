## Metrics and targets

This section summarises the metrics we use to measure and monitor sustainability-related risks and opportunities, including climate-related metrics and targets, and how we track performance against those targets.

### Metrics

We disclose:

- metrics required by applicable IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards; and
- the metrics we use to measure and monitor:
  - each sustainability-related risk or opportunity; and
  - our performance in relation to those risks and opportunities, including progress towards targets we have set and any targets required by law or regulation.

Where no IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standard specifically applies to a sustainability-related risk or opportunity, we identify applicable metrics by applying the approach in paragraphs 57–58.

Our disclosures include metrics associated with business models, activities or other common features that characterise participation in our industry.

### Sources of metrics

#### Metrics from sources other than IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards

When we disclose a metric taken from a source other than IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards, we identify:

- the source; and
- the metric taken.

For our GHG emissions metrics, the source is: [●] (for example, the GHG Protocol Corporate Accounting and Reporting Standard), and the metrics taken are Scope 1, Scope 2 (location-based and market-based) and Scope 3 emissions in tCO2e.

#### Entity-developed metrics

For any entity-developed metric we disclose:

- how the metric is defined, including whether it is derived by adjusting a metric from another source and, if so, which source and how our metric differs;
- whether the metric is absolute, a ratio, or qualitative;
- whether the metric is validated by a third party and, if so, by whom; and
- the calculation method and inputs, including significant assumptions and limitations.

### Targets

We disclose information about:

- the targets we have set to monitor progress towards achieving our strategic goals; and
- any targets we are required to meet by law or regulation.

For each target, we disclose:

- the metric used to set the target and to monitor progress;
- the specific quantitative or qualitative target;
- the period over which the target applies;
- the base period (year and value) from which progress is measured;
- milestones and interim targets;
- performance against the target in the reporting period, including trend commentary; and
- any revisions to the target and the reasons for those revisions (or that there were no revisions).

### Consistency, comparative information and changes

We apply consistent definitions and calculation methods for metrics and targets over time. If we redefine or replace a metric, we present revised comparative information unless impracticable, and we explain the change and why it provides more useful information.

We provide comparative information for the preceding period for all amounts disclosed. When a disclosed metric amount is estimated and we identify new information that provides evidence of circumstances that existed in the preceding period, we revise the comparative amount (except as specified in paragraph B51), disclose the difference, and explain the reasons for the revision.
