## Metrics and targets

This section explains the metrics and targets we use to measure and monitor sustainability-related risks and opportunities that could reasonably be expected to affect our prospects, and how those metrics and targets connect to the Governance, Strategy and Risk Management disclosures (see cross-references in the relevant sections).

**Data currency and update cadence.** Unless stated otherwise, metrics are reported for the current reporting period and are updated and disclosed annually in this Sustainability-related Financial Disclosures report.

**Reporting boundary and methodology (applies to all metrics in this section).** We define, measure, calculate and (where applicable) estimate sustainability metrics using our reporting boundary for sustainability-related financial disclosures. For each metric we specify the unit of measure and the boundary/scope (for example, organisational boundary and, where relevant, value-chain coverage). We use internal source systems and documented calculation methods; where estimation is used, we describe the key assumptions and limitations alongside the metric.

**Identification and sourcing of metrics.** The purpose of this subsection is to explain where our disclosed metrics come from and how we select them.

- For each metric taken from a source other than IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards, we identify the source and the metric taken.
- When no IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standard specifically applies to a sustainability-related risk or opportunity, we apply the relevant IFRS S1 requirements (including paragraphs 57–58) to identify applicable metrics.
- Our metrics include, where relevant, metrics associated with business models, activities or other common features that characterise participation in our industry.

**Missing or incomplete data protocol.** The purpose of this subsection is to explain how we present metrics when data is not available.

- Where a metric is **not reported for the period**, we state the status as “not reported for the period” and provide a brief reason category (for example: data not available; methodology under development; boundary not yet defined) and the planned improvement direction.

### Entity-developed metrics

The purpose of this subsection is to provide the definitions and calculation information required when we disclose metrics we have developed.

Where we disclose an entity-developed metric, we provide the following information in the metrics register (Table 1) and/or in a metric note:

- **Definition**: how the metric is defined, including whether it is derived by adjusting a metric taken from a source other than IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards and, if so, which source and how our metric differs.
- **Type of measure**: whether the metric is an absolute measure, a measure expressed in relation to another metric, or a qualitative measure.
- **Third-party validation**: whether the metric is validated by a third party and, if so, which party.
- **Calculation method**: the method used to calculate the metric and the inputs to the calculation, including significant assumptions and limitations.

### Consistency and comparability over time

The purpose of this subsection is to explain how we maintain comparability of metrics and targets over time.

- We apply metric definitions and calculation methods consistently over time.
- If we redefine or replace a metric in the reporting period, we explain what changed and why, and we provide revised comparative amounts unless impracticable.
- If we revise a previously disclosed estimated amount due to new information that provides evidence of circumstances that existed in the prior period, we disclose the revised comparative amount, the difference from the previously disclosed amount and the reasons for the revision.

### Labelling and definitions

The purpose of this subsection is to ensure metrics and targets are presented with clear, decision-useful labels.

We label and define metrics and targets using meaningful, clear and precise names and descriptions, including the unit of measure and boundary/scope in Tables 1 and 2.
