## 1 Governance

Governance arrangements support oversight of climate-related risks and opportunities through Board and committee consideration of climate-related matters, management-level committee structures, and integration of climate-related risk management into enterprise risk management (ERM).

### 1.1 Governance oversight of climate-related risks and opportunities

The Board is the primary governance body that considers climate-related risks and opportunities through its meeting agenda and discussions.

The Board comprises 10 directors. Across the reported governance records, the Board held 4, 7 and 8 full Board meetings per year. Across the reported governance records, climate-related matters were included on the Board agenda 69.8% of the time.

Board minutes evidence that Board discussions on climate-related matters include:

- transition plan review;
- scenario analysis;
- Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD) review;
- net zero progress;
- carbon credit approval; and
- executive remuneration environmental, social and governance (ESG) key performance indicators (KPIs).

Climate risk reporting is provided to the Board on a semi-annual basis.

An ESG committee is in place. Across the reported governance records, the ESG committee met 5, 6 and 7 times per year.

Climate-related considerations are incorporated into decisions on major transactions through a climate check for major transactions.

A portion of executive remuneration is linked to climate-related factors. Across the reported governance records, the proportion of executive remuneration linked to climate-related factors was 8.7%, 15.3% and 15.1%.

### 1.2 Management’s role, controls and procedures

Management oversight of climate-related risks and opportunities is delegated to a management-level committee. Across the reported governance records, the management committee identified for this purpose is recorded as:

- Group Sustainability Committee;
- ESG Executive Committee; and
- Climate Risk Management Committee.

Climate-related risk management is integrated into ERM.

### 1.3 Skills and competencies

Board climate expertise is tracked. Across the reported governance records, 33.5%, 35.5% and 37.5% of the Board was identified as having climate expertise.

A skills development programme is in place.
