- the entity’s strategy and strategic priorities;
- decisions on major transactions (where relevant); and
- the entity’s risk management processes and related policies, including integration with enterprise risk management.

Where relevant, the Board/committee considers trade-offs associated with sustainability-related and climate-related risks and opportunities as part of its review and challenge.
