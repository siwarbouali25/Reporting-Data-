## Financed emissions (Scope 3 Category 15)
This subsection provides financed emissions metrics used to monitor climate-related performance associated with lending and investment activities.

### Financed emissions — corporate loans
Financed emissions for corporate loans are calculated using the Partnership for Carbon Accounting Financials (PCAF) approach for corporate loans (PCAF Standard §B62). In plain language, the method attributes a share of a borrower’s total GHG emissions to the bank based on the bank’s **outstanding loan amount** relative to the borrower’s **enterprise value including cash (EVIC)**.

Financed emissions and the associated loan exposure are presented below.

| Reporting year | Financed emissions (loans) | Total loans | Unit |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 2022 | 36,550,177.7 | 31,150.64 | tCO₂e; €m |
| 2023 | 37,427,593.2 | 31,150.64 | tCO₂e; €m |
| 2024 | 35,973,167.7 | 31,150.64 | tCO₂e; €m |

For 2024, the bank’s financed emissions from loans were **35,973,167.7 tCO₂e**.

## Climate-related targets and progress
This subsection summarises the climate-related targets used to monitor progress, including the metric, baseline, interim milestones (where set), and progress in 2024.

### Target TGT001 — Scope 1 and Scope 2 absolute reduction
**Purpose:** This target is used to monitor progress in reducing operational emissions.

- **Scope coverage:** Scope 1 and Scope 2 (whole entity)
- **Target type:** Absolute reduction
- **Metric used to set and track the target:** tCO₂e (absolute)
- **Baseline:** 137,849.0 tCO₂e in **2020**
- **Target ambition:** **42%** reduction (percentage reduction target)
- **Interim milestones:**
  - 2025: **120,480.0 tCO₂e**
  - 2028: **97,321.4 tCO₂e**
- **Progress in 2024:** **40.0%**
- **Status:** On track
- **Third-party validation:** Validated by **SBTi**

### Scope 3 Category 15 financed emissions intensity target (sectoral decarbonisation)
**Purpose:** This target is used to monitor progress in reducing financed emissions intensity associated with lending.

- **Scope coverage:** Scope 3 Category 15 (financed emissions; whole entity)
- **Target type:** Percentage reduction
- **Metric used to set and track the target:** tCO₂e per €m lending
- **Baseline:** 20.0 tCO₂e per €m lending in **2022**
- **Target ambition:** **30%** reduction (percentage reduction target)
- **Interim milestones:**
  - 2025: **18.2 tCO₂e per €m lending**
  - 2028: **15.8 tCO₂e per €m lending**
- **Progress in 2024:** **25.0%**
- **Third-party validation:** Validated by **UNEP FI**

### All-scopes target (with planned use of carbon credits)
**Purpose:** This target is used to monitor progress across all scopes.

- **Scope coverage:** All scopes (whole entity)
- **Baseline:** 120,000.0 tCO₂e
- **Progress metric:** Percentage reduction versus baseline
- **Progress in 2024:** **13.3%**
- **Planned carbon credits:** **8.2%** of the target is planned to be met using **technology removal** carbon credits.

A total of **21,080 tCO₂e** of carbon credits is reported in the carbon credit summary.
