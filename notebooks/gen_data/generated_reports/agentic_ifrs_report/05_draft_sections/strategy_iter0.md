## Strategy

### Sustainability-related risks and opportunities that could reasonably be expected to affect prospects
The entity discloses information to enable users of general purpose financial reports to understand the sustainability-related risks and opportunities that could reasonably be expected to affect the entity’s prospects.

### Time horizons
The entity specifies the time horizons—short, medium or long term—over which the effects of each sustainability-related risk and opportunity could reasonably be expected to occur.

The entity explains how it defines ‘short term’, ‘medium term’ and ‘long term’ and how these definitions are linked to the planning horizons used for strategic decision-making.

### Business model and value chain
The entity discloses information that enables users of general purpose financial reports to understand the current and anticipated effects of sustainability-related risks and opportunities on the entity’s business model and value chain, including:

- a description of the current and anticipated effects of sustainability-related risks and opportunities on the entity’s business model and value chain; and
- a description of where in the entity’s business model and value chain sustainability-related risks and opportunities are concentrated (for example, geographical areas, facilities and types of assets).

### Strategy and decision-making
The entity discloses information that enables users of general purpose financial reports to understand the effects of sustainability-related risks and opportunities on its strategy and decision-making, including:

- how the entity has responded to, and plans to respond to, sustainability-related risks and opportunities in its strategy and decision-making;
- progress against plans disclosed in previous reporting periods, including quantitative and qualitative information; and
- trade-offs between sustainability-related risks and opportunities that the entity considered.

### Financial position, financial performance and cash flows
The entity discloses information that enables users of general purpose financial reports to understand:

- the effects of sustainability-related risks and opportunities on the entity’s financial position, financial performance and cash flows for the reporting period (current financial effects); and
- the anticipated effects of sustainability-related risks and opportunities on the entity’s financial position, financial performance and cash flows over the short, medium and long term, taking into consideration how sustainability-related risks and opportunities are included in the entity’s financial planning (anticipated financial effects).

Specifically, the entity discloses quantitative and qualitative information about:

- how sustainability-related risks and opportunities have affected its financial position, financial performance and cash flows for the reporting period;
- the sustainability-related risks and opportunities for which there is a significant risk of a material adjustment within the next annual reporting period to the carrying amounts of assets and liabilities reported in the related financial statements;
- how the entity expects its financial position to change over the short, medium and long term, given its strategy to manage sustainability-related risks and opportunities, taking into consideration:
  - its investment and disposal plans (for example, plans for capital expenditure, major acquisitions and divestments, joint ventures, business transformation, innovation, new business areas, and asset retirements), including plans the entity is not contractually committed to; and
  - its planned sources of funding to implement its strategy; and
- how the entity expects its financial performance and cash flows to change over the short, medium and long term, given its strategy to manage sustainability-related risks and opportunities.

In preparing disclosures about anticipated financial effects of a sustainability-related risk or opportunity, the entity:

- uses all reasonable and supportable information that is available at the reporting date without undue cost or effort; and
- uses an approach that is commensurate with the skills, capabilities and resources available for preparing those disclosures.
