## 1. Risk management processes and controls
This section describes the management-level structures evidenced as relevant to sustainability and climate risk management.

Management committees evidenced in the governance structure include:

- **ESG Executive Committee**
- **Climate Risk Management Committee**

Refer to [Section: Governance] for the broader governance context and how these management committees interface with oversight and decision-making.

## 2. Risk identification and assessment (climate-related)
This section summarises evidence that climate-related risk identification and assessment is supported through a climate risk register that maps to IFRS S2 risk management disclosure expectations.

Evidence from the climate risk register indicates alignment to **IFRS S2 §25(a)** across multiple entries.

Refer to [Section: Strategy] and [Section: Metrics and Targets] for related disclosures on climate-related risks and opportunities and associated measures, where applicable.
