## Governance

This section describes governance arrangements for oversight of sustainability-related risks and opportunities, including climate-related risks and opportunities.

### Oversight by governance body(ies) or individual(s)

The entity identifies the governance body(ies) (which can include a board, committee or equivalent body charged with governance) or individual(s) responsible for oversight of sustainability-related risks and opportunities, including climate-related risks and opportunities.

In particular, the entity discloses information about:

- **How responsibilities are reflected in governance documentation**—how responsibilities for sustainability-related risks and opportunities, including climate-related risks and opportunities, are reflected in the terms of reference, mandates, role descriptions and other related policies applicable to the relevant governance body(ies) or individual(s).
- **Skills and competencies**—how the governance body(ies) or individual(s) determines whether appropriate skills and competencies are available or will be developed to oversee strategies designed to respond to sustainability-related risks and opportunities, including climate-related risks and opportunities.
- **Information flows**—how and how often the governance body(ies) or individual(s) is informed about sustainability-related risks and opportunities, including climate-related risks and opportunities.
- **Consideration in oversight activities**—how the governance body(ies) or individual(s) takes into account sustainability-related risks and opportunities, including climate-related risks and opportunities, when overseeing the entity’s strategy, its decisions on major transactions and its risk management processes and related policies, including whether the governance body(ies) or individual(s) has considered trade-offs associated with those risks and opportunities.
- **Targets and remuneration linkages**—how the governance body(ies) or individual(s) oversees the setting of targets related to sustainability-related risks and opportunities, including climate-related risks and opportunities, and monitors progress towards those targets, including whether and how related performance metrics are included in remuneration policies.

### Management’s role

The entity discloses management’s role in the governance processes, controls and procedures used to monitor, manage and oversee sustainability-related risks and opportunities, including climate-related risks and opportunities, including:

- **Delegation and oversight**—whether the role is delegated to a specific management-level position or management-level committee and how oversight is exercised over that position or committee.
- **Controls, procedures and integration**—whether management uses controls and procedures to support the oversight of sustainability-related risks and opportunities, including climate-related risks and opportunities, and, if so, how these controls and procedures are integrated with other internal functions.
