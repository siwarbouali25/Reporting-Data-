Metrics are presented for the reporting year 2024, with selected multi‑year information where available. The reporting boundary for the metrics in this section is based on **financial control**, and the fiscal year end is **31 December 2024**.\n\nThe metrics and targets described below are intended to be decision‑useful for understanding (i) the entity’s operational greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions profile and (ii) the emissions associated with financing activities, including the methods used to calculate those metrics and the extent to which proxies are used.\n\n### Reporting period, boundary and consolidation\n**Reporting period:** year ended **31 December 2024**.\n\n**Reporting boundary:** **financial control**. Metrics in this section are prepared on that boundary unless otherwise indicated.\n\n### Operational GHG emissions metrics (Scopes 1, 2 and selected Scope 3)\nOperational emissions are monitored using Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions, with a selected Scope 3 category metric for business travel.\n\n#### Scope 1 (direct) GHG emissions\nFor 2024, Scope 1 emissions were **2,634.908317 tCO₂e**. The Scope 1 inventory includes fleet emissions (fleet included: **True**). A correction was applied to address errors in a pre‑computed Scope 1 value for 2024, including non‑zero values for electric vehicles.\n\n#### Scope 2 (energy indirect) GHG emissions and treatment of market‑based accounting\nFor 2024, Scope 2 emissions are monitored on both a location‑based and market‑based basis:\n\n- **Scope 2 (location‑based), 2024:** **6,963.4977 tCO₂e**\n- **Scope 2 (market‑based), 2024:** **3,060.5382 tCO₂e**\n\nThe market‑based Scope 2 figure is sourced directly from utility invoice data that already reflects market‑based accounting with grid emission factors adjusted for renewable energy certificates (RECs) and power purchase agreements (PPAs). Certificate registry data is used for disclosure narrative (for example, certificate type, volume and registry) and is not subtracted again during preparation; accordingly, the preparation approach is designed to avoid double counting.\n\n#### Scope 3 (selected category) — business travel\nFor 2024, Scope 3 business travel emissions were **289.8833 tCO₂e**. Travel activity data is available (travel data available: **True**). Comparative information for this travel metric is not indicated as available (comparative available: **False**).\n\n### Financed emissions metrics (Scope 3 Category 15)\nFinanced emissions are monitored as a key metric for a banking entity because they represent emissions associated with lending and investment activities. The financed emissions metrics below are presented for corporate loans and for listed equity investments, with additional sovereign‑related data points used in the financed emissions dataset.\n\n#### Corporate loans — financed emissions and exposure\nFinanced emissions associated with loans are measured in tCO₂e and are presented alongside the total loans exposure used in the calculation.\n\n- **2022:** financed emissions from loans **36,550,177.696418606 tCO₂e**; total loans **31,150.64 MEUR**\n- **2023:** financed emissions from loans **37,427,593.16354951 tCO₂e**; total loans **31,150.64 MEUR**\n- **2024:** financed emissions from loans **35,973,167.6878965 tCO₂e**; total loans **31,150.64 MEUR**\n\nFor 2024, the financed emissions KPI reported for the entity is **35,973,167.6878965 tCO₂e**, consistent with the financed emissions from loans metric presented above.\n\n**Methodology (corporate loans):** financed emissions for corporate loans are calculated using the following approach: **outstanding_amount_meur / evic_meur * total_ghg_tco2e (PCAF Standard §B62)**.\n\nThis metric is used to monitor the emissions associated with the lending portfolio and to support management of climate‑related risks and opportunities in lending decisions.\n\n#### Listed equity — financed emissions measurement approach and use of proxies\nListed equity positions are included in the financed emissions dataset as an asset class (**listed_equity**) and are measured using market value and related proxy inputs where direct issuer emissions are not available.\n\n**Methodology (listed equity):** **market_value_meur / issuer_evic_meur * issuer_revenue_meur (PCAF Standard §B61 proxy — revenue substituted for direct issuer emissions due to absence of counterparty-level emission data in investment records)**.\n\nFor certain listed equity positions, the proxy basis is **issuer_revenue_meur** and the proxy reason applied is: **“Direct issuer emissions unavailable. Revenue used as PCAF B61 proxy. Do not treat as verified emissions.”** This proxy approach is used to enable consistent measurement when counterparty‑level emissions are not available in investment records.\n\nSelected listed equity data points for 2024 include (currency: **EUR**):\n\n- Market value **23.93 MEUR**; nominal amount **27.98 MEUR**; purchase date **2019‑05‑26**; proxy basis **issuer_revenue_meur**\n- Market value **556.4 MEUR**; nominal amount **636.77 MEUR**; proxy basis **issuer_revenue_meur**\n- Market value **35.36 MEUR**; proxy basis **issuer_revenue_meur**\n- Market value **79.11 MEUR**; proxy basis **issuer_revenue_meur**\n- Market value **25.57 MEUR**; proxy basis **issuer_revenue_meur**\n- Market value **1,886.33 MEUR**; proxy basis **issuer_revenue_meur**\n- Market value **2,564.65 MEUR**; proxy basis **issuer_revenue_meur**\n- Market value **288.08 MEUR**; proxy basis **issuer_revenue_meur**\n\nThese data points support the measurement of financed emissions for listed equity using the stated PCAF proxy methodology.\n\n#### Sovereign-related financed emissions dataset inputs\nThe financed emissions dataset includes sovereign‑related inputs, including national Scope 1 emissions values (tCO₂e) and selected purchase dates for sovereign positions. Examples of national Scope 1 values included in the dataset are **675,000,000.0 tCO₂e**, **290,000,000.0 tCO₂e**, **330,000,000.0 tCO₂e**, and **150,000,000.0 tCO₂e**. Selected purchase dates included in the dataset include **2022‑01‑30**, **2022‑03‑18**, **2022‑08‑22**, **2022‑11‑15**, **2023‑05‑14**, **2023‑12‑26**, **2024‑04‑16**, and **2024‑11‑16**.\n\n### Portfolio exposure metric — high carbon sector exposure\nThe entity monitors exposure to high carbon sectors using both an absolute exposure metric and a percentage metric:\n\n- **High carbon sector exposure:** **11,059.49 MEUR**\n- **High carbon sector exposure (percentage):** **35.5%**\n\nThese metrics are used to support monitoring of portfolio composition in relation to climate‑related transition risk.\n\n### Data quality and estimation mix for emissions metrics\nTo support transparency over the reliability of emissions information used in decision‑making, the entity summarises the mix of emissions data quality approaches used in its emissions dataset:\n\n- **Estimated (economic): 47.5%**\n- **CDP disclosure: 35.5%**\n- **Proxy model: 16.3%**\n- **Audited report: 0.7%**\n\nThis mix indicates the extent to which financed emissions and related metrics rely on estimates and proxies versus audited or directly reported sources.\n\n### Internal carbon price\nAn internal carbon price is used as an input to decision‑making in specified applications. The internal carbon price is expressed in **EUR per tCO₂e** and is applied as follows:\n\n- **Investment decisions:** **EUR 42.73 per tCO₂e**; applies to financed emissions (**True**) and applies to lending decisions (**True**)\n- **Product pricing:** **EUR 48.73 per tCO₂e**; applies to financed emissions (**False**) and applies to lending decisions (**False**)\n- **Investment decisions:** **EUR 54.73 per tCO₂e**; applies to financed emissions (**False**) and applies to lending decisions (**True**)\n\nThese internal carbon prices are used to incorporate a carbon cost signal into relevant decision processes, including where indicated for financed emissions and lending decisions.\n\n### Climate-related targets\nTargets are used to manage performance against climate‑related objectives. Targets are defined by scope coverage, metric, baseline, target type, interim milestones, and progress.\n\n#### Target TGT001 — Scope 1 and Scope 2 absolute reduction target\n**Purpose:** This target is used to manage operational emissions (Scopes 1 and 2) across the whole entity.\n\n- **Target ID:** **TGT001**\n- **Scope coverage:** **Scope 1 and 2** (scope: **scope_1_and_2**; scope_coverage: **scope1_and_2**)\n- **Target type:** **absolute reduction**\n- **Metric:** **tCO₂e** (progress metric: **tco2e_absolute**)\n- **Baseline year:** **2020**\n- **Baseline value:** **137,849.0 tCO₂e**\n- **Target value:** **42% reduction**\n- **Interim milestones:**\n  - **2025:** **120,480.0 tCO₂e**\n  - **2028:** **97,321.4 tCO₂e**\n- **Target applies to:** **whole entity**\n- **Status (2024):** **on_track**\n- **Progress (2024):** **40.0%**\n- **Third‑party validated:** **True**; validation body: **SBTi**\n\nThis target provides a structured pathway for reducing operational emissions, with interim milestones supporting monitoring of progress over time.\n\n#### Financed emissions intensity target — Scope 3 Category 15 (financed)\n**Purpose:** This target is used to manage financed emissions performance through an intensity metric linked to lending.\n\n- **Scope coverage:** **Scope 3 Category 15** (scope: **scope_3_cat15_financed**; scope_coverage: **scope3_cat15**)\n- **Target type:** reduction target (target value: **30% reduction**)\n- **Metric:** **tCO₂e per MEUR lending** (metric: **tco2e_per_meur_lending**; progress metric: **tco2e_per_meur**)\n- **Baseline year:** **2022**\n- **Baseline value:** **20.0** (tCO₂e per MEUR lending)\n- **Interim milestones:**\n  - **2025:** **18.2 tCO₂e per MEUR lending**\n  - **2028:** **15.8 tCO₂e per MEUR lending**\n- **Target applies to:** **whole entity**\n- **Sectoral decarbonisation:** **True**\n- **Progress (2024):** **25.0%**\n- **Third‑party validated:** **True**; validation body: **UNEP_FI**\n\nFor monitoring purposes, the entity also tracks a financed emissions intensity reference value of **20.0 tCO₂e per MEUR**.\n\n#### All-scopes target (including planned use of carbon credits)\n**Purpose:** This target is used to manage progress across all scopes using a percentage reduction versus baseline.\n\n- **Scope coverage:** **all scopes** (scope: **all_scopes**; scope_coverage: **all_scopes**)\n- **Progress metric:** **percentage reduction versus baseline** (progress metric: **pct_reduction_vs_baseline**)\n- **Baseline value:** **120,000.0**\n- **Associated gross target ID:** **TGT001**\n- **Planned carbon credits:** **8.2%** of the target plan; planned credit type: **technology_removal**\n- **Sectoral decarbonisation:** **False**\n- **Target applies to:** **whole entity**\n- **Progress (2024):** **13.3%**\n- **Third‑party validated:** **True**\n\nIn addition to planned use within the target plan, the entity reports a carbon credit total of **21,080 tCO₂e**.\n\n### Connectivity to decision-making\nThe metrics and targets in this section are used to support decision‑making and monitoring, including through the use of an internal carbon price in investment decisions and, where indicated, lending decisions. Financed emissions metrics and the high carbon sector exposure metrics provide inputs for assessing portfolio‑related climate considerations, while operational emissions metrics and the Scope 1 and 2 target support monitoring of operational decarbonisation progress.\n",
