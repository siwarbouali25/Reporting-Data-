## Governance

This section describes how climate-related and broader sustainability-related risks and opportunities are overseen and managed, including the roles of the Board and management committees, the cadence and flow of climate risk reporting, and how competence and remuneration are linked to climate-related performance.

### Board oversight and agenda integration

The Board provides oversight of climate-related and sustainability-related risks and opportunities through its regular governance cycle. The Board comprises **10 directors** and holds **four full Board meetings per year**.

Climate-related matters are integrated into Board deliberations and decision-making. Across the reporting period, climate-related topics were included on the Board agenda in **69.8%** of Board agenda items. Board discussions evidenced in meeting records included review of the **transition plan**, **scenario analysis**, review aligned to **TCFD**, monitoring of **net zero progress**, consideration of **executive remuneration ESG KPIs**, and **approval of carbon credits**.

These agenda items demonstrate that the Board’s oversight extends beyond periodic updates and includes review of climate-related strategy and progress monitoring, as well as consideration of climate-related instruments and incentives.

### Climate risk reporting to the Board

To support effective oversight, climate risk reporting is provided to the Board on a **semi-annual** basis. This reporting cadence is designed to ensure the Board is informed about climate-related risks and opportunities with sufficient frequency to oversee climate-related strategy, monitor progress, and consider climate-related implications in key decisions.

The Board’s climate-related oversight is supported by an ESG committee structure. An **ESG committee** is in place and met **five times per year** in the earliest period evidenced, increasing to **six** and then **seven meetings per year** in subsequent periods. This committee meeting cadence provides an additional governance forum to review climate-related and sustainability-related matters and to support escalation to the Board as required.

### Oversight of strategy, targets and progress monitoring

Board oversight includes review of climate-related strategy and progress against climate-related objectives. Board meeting records show that the Board considered:

- **Transition plan review**, including updates and oversight of the plan’s direction.
- **Scenario analysis**, supporting Board understanding of climate-related uncertainties and potential pathways.
- **Net zero progress**, indicating ongoing monitoring of progress.
- **TCFD-aligned review**, reflecting Board engagement with climate-related disclosure and governance expectations.

The Board also considered how climate-related performance is reflected in executive incentives through discussion of **executive remuneration ESG KPIs**. This linkage supports oversight of climate-related targets and progress by embedding climate-related performance considerations into remuneration outcomes.

### Consideration of climate in major transactions

Climate-related considerations are embedded into governance over major transactions. A **climate check is applied to major transactions**, supporting Board oversight of how climate-related risks and opportunities are taken into account when evaluating significant decisions.

In addition, Board meeting records show **approval of carbon credits** was considered at Board level. This indicates that climate-related instruments can be subject to Board-level review and decision-making where relevant.

### Management’s role and committee structure

Management’s role in monitoring, managing and overseeing climate-related and sustainability-related risks and opportunities is delegated to a management-level committee structure. The management committee responsible for this oversight is evidenced as operating under the following committee names across the periods presented:

- **Group Sustainability Committee**
- **ESG Executive Committee**
- **Climate Risk Management Committee**

These management committees provide a structured mechanism for coordinating climate-related and sustainability-related activities, supporting implementation of climate-related strategy, and enabling escalation of relevant matters through governance channels.

### Integration with enterprise risk management (ERM)

Climate-related and sustainability-related risks and opportunities are integrated into the organisation’s **enterprise risk management (ERM)** framework. This integration supports consistent identification, assessment and oversight of climate-related risks and opportunities alongside other principal risks, and provides a basis for structured reporting and governance escalation.

### Skills, competencies and capability development

The Board’s oversight is supported by climate-related competence within the governance structure. **37.5%** of the Board is assessed as having climate expertise (with earlier evidenced levels of **33.5%** and **35.5%**).

A **skills development programme** is in place to support the development and maintenance of skills and competencies relevant to oversight of climate-related and sustainability-related risks and opportunities. This programme supports the Board and governance bodies in maintaining appropriate capability to oversee strategies designed to respond to climate-related risks and opportunities.

### Remuneration linkage to climate-related performance

Climate-related performance metrics are incorporated into executive remuneration through ESG KPI linkages. The proportion of executive remuneration linked to climate-related considerations is evidenced as:

- **8.7%** in the earliest period presented
- **15.3%** in a subsequent period
- **15.1%** in the most recent period presented

This remuneration linkage is reinforced by Board-level discussion of **executive remuneration ESG KPIs**, supporting governance oversight of how climate-related performance is embedded into incentives.

### Governance maturity indicators (selected)

The following indicators summarise selected governance characteristics evidenced in the reporting period:

- **Board climate expertise:** **37.5%**
- **ESG committee meetings per year:** **7**
- **ERM integration:** climate-related and sustainability-related risks and opportunities are integrated into ERM

These indicators are intended to provide decision-useful insight into the governance arrangements supporting oversight and management of climate-related and sustainability-related risks and opportunities.
