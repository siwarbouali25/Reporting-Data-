### Methodology for financed emissions (PCAF)
Financed emissions for **corporate loans** are calculated in line with the Partnership for Carbon Accounting Financials (PCAF) approach referenced in the PCAF Standard (§B62). In plain terms, the Bank attributes a share of a borrower’s total greenhouse gas emissions to the Bank based on the Bank’s share of the borrower’s enterprise value including cash (EVIC). The attribution factor is derived from the **outstanding loan amount** relative to the borrower’s **EVIC**, and this factor is applied to the borrower’s **total GHG emissions** to estimate the Bank’s financed emissions for that exposure.

### Listed equity exposure (measurement inputs and proxy approach)
For **listed equity** positions reported for 2024, the Bank applies a PCAF proxy approach referenced in the PCAF Standard (§B61). Under this approach, **issuer revenue** is used as the proxy input in the calculation, together with the Bank’s **market value** of the holding and the issuer’s **EVIC**.

## Targets and progress
This subsection summarises climate-related targets used to monitor progress, including the metrics used, base periods, interim milestones, and progress in 2024.

### Target: Scope 1 and Scope 2 absolute emissions reduction (TGT001)
This target is used to monitor progress in reducing operational emissions.

- **Scope coverage:** Scope 1 and Scope 2
- **Target type:** Absolute reduction
- **Metric used to set and monitor the target:** Total emissions in **tCO₂e** (absolute)
- **Base year and baseline value:** **2020 baseline of 137,849 tCO₂e**
- **Target ambition:** **42% reduction**
- **Interim milestones:**
  - **2025:** 120,480 tCO₂e
  - **2028:** 97,321.4 tCO₂e
- **Progress in 2024:** **40.0%**
- **Status:** On track
- **Third-party validation:** Validated by **SBTi**

### Target: Financed emissions intensity reduction (Scope 3 Category 15)
This target is used to monitor progress in reducing financed emissions intensity associated with the Bank’s financing activities.

- **Scope coverage:** Scope 3 Category 15 (financed emissions)
- **Metric used to set and monitor the target:** **tCO₂e per EUR million of lending**
- **Base year and baseline value:** **2022 baseline of 20.0 tCO₂e per EUR million of lending**
- **Target ambition:** **30% reduction**
- **Interim milestones:**
  - **2025:** 18.2 tCO₂e per EUR million of lending
  - **2028:** 15.8 tCO₂e per EUR million of lending
- **Progress in 2024:** **25.0%**
- **Third-party validation:** Validated by **UNEP FI**

### Target: All-scopes target (including planned use of carbon credits)
This target is used to monitor progress across all scopes.

- **Scope coverage:** All scopes
- **Progress metric:** Percentage reduction versus baseline
- **Baseline value:** 120,000 (all Kyoto 7 gases)
- **Progress in 2024:** **13.3%**
- **Planned use of carbon credits:** **8.2%** of the target is planned to be met using **technology removal** carbon credits.

In addition, the Bank reports a total carbon credit volume of **21,080 tCO₂e**.
