## 1. Risk management processes and controls
This subsection describes the management committees evidenced in the governance structure that support oversight and coordination of sustainability-related and climate-related risk management activities.

Management committees evidenced in the governance structure include:
- **Group Sustainability Committee**
- **ESG Executive Committee**
- **Climate Risk Management Committee**

These committees provide governance forums through which sustainability-related and climate-related risk topics may be reviewed and escalated through the governance structure, as applicable. Refer to [Section: Governance].

## 2. Risk identification and assessment (climate-related)
This subsection describes the climate risk register and summarises the evidence it contains regarding referenced IFRS S2 paragraphs.

**Climate risk register (definition).** The climate risk register is a record used to document climate-related risk items and associated references to relevant disclosure requirements.

Evidence from the climate risk register indicates that multiple entries reference **IFRS S2 §25(a)**. See [Section: Strategy] and [Section: Metrics and Targets].
